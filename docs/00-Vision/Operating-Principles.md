## Purpose

These principles define how Encore is designed, built and evolved.

They are intended to guide every strategic, product and engineering decision.

Every contributor should understand these principles before proposing significant changes to the platform.

## Our Leadership Model

Encore is built through three complementary roles.

### Founder & CEO

Owns:

- Company vision
- Commercial strategy
- Customer relationships
- Partnerships
- Product priorities
- Final decisions

Primary Question:

"Is this the right decision for the business?"

### Chief Product & Strategy Architect

Owns:

- Product vision
- User experience
- AI strategy
- Product documentation
- Audience journey
- Long-term platform strategy

Primary Question:

"Are we building the right product?"

### Engineering Lead

Owns:

- Software architecture
- Engineering quality
- Security
- Performance
- Testing
- Delivery
- Technical documentation

Primary Question:

"Are we building it the right way?"

## Decision Framework

Every significant initiative should pass through three stages.

1. Strategic Review
2. Engineering Review
3. Founder Approval

No significant feature should proceed without considering all three perspectives.

## Product Before Technology

Technology exists to serve the product.

The product exists to serve the vision.

The vision exists to serve the customer.

Never allow technology to dictate product direction.

## Authority Through Verification

Platform authority should always be earned through objective verification rather than simple account creation.

Identity grants access.

Verification grants authority.

An Encore account does not automatically grant review permissions. A Verified Review Invitation following verified attendance grants the bounded authority to contribute a review.

Future contribution features must define their verification mechanism before implementation.

> **At Encore, anyone can discover. Only people who were there can contribute.**

## Challenge Assumptions

Healthy disagreement is encouraged.

Ideas should be challenged respectfully.

The goal is always to strengthen the platform.

Agreement should come from evidence and reasoning rather than hierarchy.

## Simplicity Wins

Prefer simple solutions over complex ones.

Every feature should solve a clearly defined problem.

Complexity should only be introduced when it creates measurable value.

## AI Principles

AI is an enabling capability.

It is not the product.

Every AI feature should demonstrably improve:

- Discovery
- Insight
- Decision-making
- Audience experience

If AI does not improve one of these areas, it should not be implemented.

## Ecosystem Thinking

Every decision should strengthen the overall ecosystem.

TicketPal should make Encore stronger.

Encore should make TicketPal stronger.

Both products must continue to provide independent value.

Together they should provide a competitive advantage that neither product could achieve alone.

## Open Platform

Encore is designed as an open platform.

We compete through innovation, intelligence and trust rather than platform lock-in.

TicketPal provides the flagship native integration but Encore should remain provider-agnostic.

## Documentation First

Major product and architecture decisions should be documented before implementation.

Documentation is considered part of the product.

It should evolve alongside the platform.

## Engineering Principles

Engineering should:

- Raise concerns early.
- Protect long-term maintainability.
- Avoid unnecessary technical debt.
- Preserve architectural consistency.
- Explain trade-offs clearly.

Implementation quality is as important as feature delivery.

## Product Guardian

Before implementing any significant feature ask:

- Does this strengthen trust?
- Does this improve discovery?
- Does this generate meaningful audience intelligence?
- Does this help someone make a better decision?
- Does this support the Audience Journey?
- Does this align with the Platform Manifesto?
- Does contribution authority derive from explicit verification rather than identity alone?

If the answer is unclear, raise the concern before implementation.
