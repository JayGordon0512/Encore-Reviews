# ADR-000: Founding Principles

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-24
- Scope: Product, engineering, integrations, and ecosystem governance

## Context

Encore requires an enduring governance decision that aligns company strategy, product direction, engineering delivery, and the relationship between Encore and TicketPal as the platform grows.

The governing operating model is defined by the [Operating Principles](../00-Vision/Operating-Principles.md). This ADR records the founding ecosystem decision that all future capabilities must respect.

## Decision

Every feature must strengthen the ecosystem, not simply an individual product.

## Consequences

- TicketPal and Encore must each provide standalone value.
- Together they create a strategic competitive advantage.
- New capabilities should benefit the ecosystem wherever practical.
- Long-term product coherence is prioritised over short-term feature delivery.

## Governance

Significant initiatives follow the Strategic Review, Engineering Review, and Founder Approval stages defined in the [Operating Principles](../00-Vision/Operating-Principles.md).

A future decision that changes this founding principle must be discussed explicitly and recorded in a superseding ADR. It must not be changed implicitly through implementation or by editing this record in place.
