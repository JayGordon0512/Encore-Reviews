# Encore v1.3.0 — Organiser Audience Invitations

Encore exists to orchestrate the live entertainment ecosystem through trusted experiences and collective intelligence.

- Status: Implemented locally; activation remains independently gated
- Date: 30 August 2026
- Scope: Encore-owned manual events and organiser-confirmed CSV attendance

## Release outcome

Independent organisers can import customers against one event performance and
have Encore create one secure invitation schedule for each newly confirmed
attendance. The existing encrypted contact store, identifier-only queue job,
fragment/POST/session invitation entry and single-use review transaction are
reused without introducing a ticketing provider into the organiser-owned path.

## Ownership and isolation

- `AudienceAttendance` is the contribution authority for an organiser import.
- Provider v2 eligibility remains a separate authority and retains its own gate.
- A schedule references either provider eligibility or organiser attendance;
  the issuing service resolves the correct authority from its explicit source.
- Tenant-owned show and performance constraints remain unchanged.
- An attendance can produce at most one active usable invitation, including
  during retries and concurrent delivery attempts.

## Activation

Organiser invitation scheduling is controlled by
`ENCORE_ORGANISER_INVITATION_ISSUING_ENABLED`. It is independent of
`ENCORE_PROVIDER_V2_INVITATION_ISSUING_ENABLED`.

Keep the organiser gate disabled until the target environment has:

1. a supervised scheduler process;
2. a supervised worker for the `invitations` queue;
3. working Encore mail credentials and sender authentication;
4. queue, failed-job and dead-letter monitoring;
5. a controlled CSV import that produces one redeemable email per new row.

`ENCORE_ORGANISER_INVITATION_DELAY_HOURS` controls the delay after the selected
performance. If an organiser confirms attendance after that point, the schedule
becomes due immediately.

## Verification

Feature coverage proves encrypted and deduplicated import, disabled-state
suppression, independent organiser activation while provider issuing is off,
identifier-only dispatch, successful mail delivery, source attribution and one
invitation per confirmed attendance. Existing provider delivery, failure,
replacement-token and secure redemption coverage remains green.
