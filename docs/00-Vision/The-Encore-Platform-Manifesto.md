## Why We Exist

Live entertainment creates moments that stay with us for a lifetime.

Every performance, concert, play, musical, comedy show and community event represents countless hours of creativity, passion and dedication. Yet when the curtain falls, most organisations are left with only one question:

“How many tickets did we sell?”

Ticket sales tell us what happened.

They do not tell us why audiences connected with a performance.

They do not reveal what inspired standing ovations, what moved people emotionally, why audiences return, or what could make the next production even better.

We believe the live entertainment industry deserves more than transactional data.

It deserves understanding.

Encore exists to provide that understanding.

⸻

## Our Vision

To become the Audience Intelligence Platform for Live Entertainment.

We are not building another review website.

We are building the intelligence layer that connects audiences, organisers, venues and producers through trusted insight.

Our purpose is to help organisations make better decisions, create stronger productions, build lasting communities and connect more people with unforgettable live experiences.

⸻

## Our Mission

To transform verified audience experiences into actionable intelligence that helps audiences discover unforgettable performances and empowers organisations to understand, grow and engage their communities.

Everything we build should serve this mission.

⸻

## The Opportunity

Today’s entertainment platforms focus on transactions.

Encore focuses on relationships.

Ticketing platforms answer questions like:

* Who bought a ticket?
* When was it purchased?
* What seat was selected?

Encore answers far more valuable questions:

* Who attended?
* What did they experience?
* Why did they enjoy it?
* Would they recommend it?
* Who is likely to return?
* What should they discover next?
* How can organisations create even better experiences?

That knowledge becomes the foundation for smarter decisions across the live entertainment industry.

⸻

## What We Believe

We believe trust is everything.

Every insight begins with genuine audience experiences.

Verified attendance.

Verified reviews.

Verified engagement.

Without trust there can be no meaningful intelligence.

### The Authority Principle

> **At Encore, anyone can discover. Only people who were there can contribute.**

- Identity grants access.
- Verification grants authority.
- Accounts do not automatically grant review permissions.
- Every review originates from verified attendance.
- Audience Intelligence is built from verified experiences.
- AI insights inherit their trust from verified audience data.

An Encore account represents who a person is.

A Verified Review Invitation represents what they are authorised to contribute.

These concepts must remain separate throughout the platform.

⸻

We believe every audience interaction has value.

Every review.

Every recommendation.

Every return visit.

Every first-time attendee.

Every shared experience.

Every audience member teaches us something.

When combined responsibly these experiences become knowledge.

Knowledge leads to better decisions.

⸻

We believe reviews are only the beginning.

Reviews are not our product.

Reviews are the mechanism through which trusted audience intelligence is created.

The real value lies in the insight generated from those experiences.

⸻

We believe discovery should be personal.

The perfect show already exists.

Our job is helping the right audience discover it.

Encore should become the trusted companion that introduces people to performances they genuinely love—not simply those with the largest marketing budgets.

⸻

We believe marketing should become intelligent.

Marketing should never be about sending more messages.

It should be about sending better messages to the right people at the right time.

Helping organisations reach audiences who are genuinely interested benefits everyone.

Better targeting.

Less waste.

Stronger communities.

Fuller theatres.

⸻

We believe data only has value when it creates action.

Dashboards are not the goal.

Reports are not the goal.

Data is not the goal.

The goal is helping people make better decisions.

Every insight Encore provides should answer one simple question:

“What should I do next?”

⸻

We believe Artificial Intelligence should be invisible.

AI is not our product.

AI is the intelligence engine that quietly transforms information into understanding.

We will never build AI because it is fashionable.

We will only build AI when it genuinely improves discovery, insight or decision-making.

If it does not create measurable value, it does not belong in Encore.

⸻

We believe privacy creates trust.

Audience trust is our greatest asset.

People will always control how their information is used.

Data should always be collected transparently, ethically and with informed consent.

People are never the product.

Their experience is.

## Open by Design

Encore supports the wider live entertainment ecosystem.

Organisations should be able to use Encore without changing their ticketing provider.

Encore remains independent and provider-agnostic, competing through product quality, intelligence and trust rather than platform lock-in.

TicketPal is the flagship native integration and should provide the richest, deepest and most seamless Encore experience.

That native advantage comes from integration quality, not exclusivity.

Encore and TicketPal must each provide standalone value. Together they should create a strategic advantage that neither product could achieve alone.

## Our Principles

Every feature we build should achieve at least one of these goals:

* Increase trust.
* Improve discovery.
* Generate meaningful insight.
* Strengthen audience relationships.
* Help organisations sell more tickets ethically.
* Respect audience privacy and informed consent.
* Make sophisticated intelligence simple to understand.
* Create measurable value for everyone in the ecosystem.

If a feature does not clearly support these principles, we should question whether it belongs in Encore.
