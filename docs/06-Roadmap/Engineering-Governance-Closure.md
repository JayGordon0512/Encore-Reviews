# Engineering Governance Closure

Encore exists to orchestrate the live entertainment ecosystem through trusted experiences and collective intelligence.

This document contributes to that purpose.

- Status: **Deferred**
- Programme type: Engineering governance and operational readiness
- Scheduling status: Unscheduled
- Current priority: **Encore Product Design** and the **Showcase Release**
- Owner: Encore Reviews Engineering
- Last reviewed: 16 July 2026

## Purpose

This deferred programme captures the remaining governance and documentation work identified when closing the v0.3.0 Enterprise Foundation Programme. It is a backlog record only and does not authorize implementation, repository changes, external coordination, or creation of a GitHub issue.

The Enterprise Foundation remains complete and authoritative. Deferring this programme does not weaken the [v0.3.0 architectural baseline](../07-Releases/v0.3.0-Enterprise-Foundation.md) or its approval gates.

## Deferral decision

Current delivery priority has shifted to **Encore Product Design** and the **Showcase Release**. Engineering Governance Closure will remain deferred until those priorities permit scheduling and an accountable owner explicitly moves this programme into active planning.

No work item should be inferred from this document. A GitHub issue may be created later when the programme is ready to be scheduled; it must not be created automatically.

## Deferred scope

The future programme may address:

- a formal Architecture Review template and durable approval record;
- resolution and approval of outstanding Provider API v2 contract decisions;
- production deployment, rollback, backup, restore, incident-response, and observability runbooks;
- release tagging and automated release-note governance;
- a machine-readable provider contract such as OpenAPI after contract approval;
- named operational owners, escalation paths, and service levels;
- documentation linting and cross-reference verification in continuous integration;
- closure or rescheduling of governance gaps recorded in the Enterprise Foundation release.

These are scope placeholders, not approved designs or committed deliverables.

## Reactivation criteria

The programme may move out of Deferred status only when:

1. Encore Product Design and Showcase Release priorities allow capacity.
2. A programme owner and stakeholders are named.
3. Scope, outcomes, dependencies, and acceptance criteria are reviewed.
4. Any architectural work follows the Platform Baseline approval requirements.
5. A GitHub issue or other scheduling record is created deliberately by the team.

## Current recommendation

Retain this document as the single backlog reference for Engineering Governance Closure. Do not begin implementation or create tracking issues until the programme is explicitly scheduled.
