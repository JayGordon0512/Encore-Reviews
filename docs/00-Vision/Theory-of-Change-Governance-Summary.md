# Theory of Change Governance Summary

Encore exists to orchestrate the live entertainment ecosystem through trusted experiences and collective intelligence.

This document contributes to that purpose.

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Product Guardian governance review

**Review date:** 24 July 2026

## Outcome

The [Encore Theory of Change](THEORY-OF-CHANGE.md) is aligned with the [Encore Core Purpose](CORE-PURPOSE.md) and the existing strategic foundation.

It does not redefine Encore's purpose. It explains how orchestration, verified audience experience and collective intelligence are expected to create positive change across the live entertainment ecosystem.

## Terminology Decision

The constitutional Purpose uses **collective intelligence**: knowledge created from many verified human experiences. Shared intelligence describes the appropriate distribution and application of that collective knowledge across the ecosystem.

These terms are complementary and should not be treated as competing platform purposes.

## Recommendations for Discussion

1. Add orchestration and collective-intelligence outcomes when the Manifesto, Platform Strategy and Product Blueprint next receive approved revisions.
2. Define evidence standards for proving that a feature contributes to ecosystem change rather than only local product activity.
3. Validate the direct outcomes expected by venues, artists and industry partners before creating new product commitments.
4. Clarify the Platform Charter's current-product scope so it cannot be mistaken for the enduring definition of Encore.
5. Establish representation, privacy and provenance standards before describing aggregated audience experience as collective intelligence.

No application or architecture changes are authorised by this review.
