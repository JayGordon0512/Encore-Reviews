# Encore Audience Journey

Encore exists to orchestrate the live entertainment ecosystem through trusted experiences and collective intelligence.

This document contributes to that purpose.

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Authoritative product vision

**Owner:** Chief Product & Strategy Architect

## Purpose

This document translates the [Encore Core Purpose](CORE-PURPOSE.md) into the intended audience relationship with Encore from open discovery through attendance, verified contribution, optional membership, personalisation, and repeat discovery.

It protects one foundational rule:

> **At Encore, anyone can discover. Only people who were there can contribute.**

Account identity, public access, consent, and contribution authority are separate throughout the journey.

## Journey Principles

### Discovery Is Open

Anyone can discover, browse, read approved reviews, and follow ticket links without creating an Encore account.

### Booking Happens Through Supported Providers

Encore connects audiences with supported ticketing providers. Encore does not require an organisation or audience member to use TicketPal, although TicketPal provides the flagship native experience.

### Attendance Creates Potential Authority

Booking or holding an account does not itself authorize a review. Attendance must be verified through an approved evidence path before review authority is granted.

### Verification Grants Authority

A Verified Review Invitation represents explicit authority to contribute one trusted review for the verified performance and subject to its lifecycle rules.

### Contribution Does Not Require Membership

An eligible audience member should be able to use a valid Verified Review Invitation without first creating an Encore account.

### Membership Is Optional and Valuable

After contributing, an audience member may create My Encore to keep history, control preferences, and receive ongoing discovery value. Declining membership must not invalidate the contribution.

### Trust Is Visible

The journey should explain why a contribution is permitted, what “verified” means, how publication works, and what the audience member can do when something goes wrong.

## Primary Journey

```text
Discover
↓
Book
↓
Attend
↓
Attendance Verified
↓
Verified Review Invitation
↓
Review Submitted
↓
(Optional)
Create My Encore
↓
Unlock personalisation and ongoing membership benefits
```

The review authority exists because attendance was verified, not because an account was created.

## Journey Stages

### 1. Discover

**Audience goal:** Find live entertainment that feels relevant, trustworthy, and worth considering.

**Encore experience:**

- browse shows, performances, venues, and organisations;
- read approved verified reviews;
- understand scores and recommendation rates;
- search or filter by useful public attributes;
- receive non-personalised discovery without an account.

**Trust promise:** Public content uses clear publication and verification rules.

**Account requirement:** None.

### 2. Decide

**Audience goal:** Decide whether a performance fits their interests, needs, location, timing, and confidence threshold.

**Encore experience:**

- see accurate performance and venue context;
- compare evidence and review themes;
- understand ticket-provider destination;
- distinguish organic relevance from any commercial placement;
- access appropriate accessibility information where available.

**Trust promise:** Encore does not manufacture consensus or hide the basis of a recommendation.

**Account requirement:** None.

### 3. Book

**Audience goal:** Purchase through a supported ticketing provider with clear continuity from discovery.

**Encore experience:**

- follow an accurate ticket link;
- understand which provider will complete the transaction;
- retain confidence that Encore remains independent of the provider;
- receive the richest native continuity when booking through TicketPal where supported.

**Trust promise:** Provider choice does not determine the honesty of reviews or public ranking.

**Account requirement:** Determined by the ticketing provider, not by Encore discovery.

### 4. Attend

**Audience goal:** Experience the booked performance.

**Encore experience:** Encore remains unobtrusive. Attendance context is supplied or confirmed only through approved provider or verification paths.

**Trust promise:** Attendance and identity information is used only for approved purposes and protected according to the applicable relationship.

**Account requirement:** None from Encore.

### 5. Attendance Verified

**Audience goal:** Be recognised as eligible to contribute without unnecessary friction or exposure.

**Encore experience:**

- receive a clear indication that attendance evidence has been accepted;
- associate eligibility with the correct performance;
- avoid silent reliance on account membership as proof;
- provide a correction or support path when evidence is missing or wrong.

**Trust promise:** Verification establishes eligibility and provenance; it does not determine what opinion the audience member may express.

**Account requirement:** None.

### 6. Verified Review Invitation

**Audience goal:** Understand what they are invited and authorized to contribute.

**Encore experience:**

- invitation linked to the verified performance;
- clear expiry, single-use, and identity-match behavior where applicable;
- explanation of verification, review expectations, privacy, and moderation;
- accessible route into review submission;
- no requirement to create an Encore account first.

**Trust promise:** The invitation grants bounded authority for a trusted contribution. It is not a general account permission.

**Account requirement:** None.

### 7. Review Submitted

**Audience goal:** Share an honest experience and understand what happens next.

**Encore experience:**

- submit rating, recommendation, tags, and optional review content;
- consume the invitation authority once;
- confirm the correct performance and verification state;
- explain moderation and publication status;
- provide correction, withdrawal, support, or appeal routes as policy permits.

**Trust promise:** Verification protects eligibility. Publication governance protects the platform. Neither should rewrite legitimate opinion merely because it is critical.

**Account requirement:** None.

### 8. Optional: Create My Encore

**Audience goal:** Preserve history and receive continued value from Encore.

**Encore experience:**

- offer account creation after review submission without coercion;
- explain membership benefits clearly;
- link the contribution to the account only through an approved, secure process;
- keep consent choices separate from account creation;
- allow the audience member to decline without affecting the submitted review.

**Trust promise:** Identity grants access to membership features. It does not retrospectively create or expand contribution authority.

### 9. Personalise

**Audience goal:** Shape discovery around declared interests and chosen activity.

**Encore experience:**

- save favourites;
- follow venues and organisations;
- maintain watchlists;
- view review and entertainment history;
- set location and discovery preferences;
- receive explainable personal recommendations;
- inspect, reset, or disable personalisation.

**Trust promise:** Personalisation and direct marketing are separate choices.

### 10. Return and Engage

**Audience goal:** Discover another valuable live experience without intrusive contact.

**Encore experience:**

- requested favourite and watchlist alerts;
- selected venue or organisation updates;
- optional weekly discovery;
- similar-show or personal recommendations;
- frequency and channel controls;
- a clear route back to public discovery.

**Trust promise:** Engagement optimizes relevance and audience value, not contact volume.

## The Review Stage

### Authority Sequence

The authoritative review journey is:

```text
Discover
↓
Book
↓
Attend
↓
Attendance Verified
↓
Verified Review Invitation
↓
Review Submitted
↓
(Optional)
Create My Encore
↓
Unlock personalisation and ongoing membership benefits
```

### Prohibited Journey Assumptions

The product must not imply that:

- creating an account grants review permission;
- booking alone always proves attendance;
- an organisation administrator may manually treat account ownership as attendance evidence;
- an expired, used, revoked, or mismatched invitation remains authoritative;
- linking a review to an account changes the original verification provenance;
- account deletion automatically falsifies the historic fact that a review was verified.

## Optional Membership Journey

Creating My Encore should be offered when the audience member can understand the immediate value.

Membership may provide:

- saved favourites;
- followed venues;
- followed organisations;
- personal recommendations;
- entertainment history;
- review history;
- watchlists;
- notifications and channel preferences;
- privacy, consent, and data controls.

Membership should not be required to:

- browse public shows;
- read approved reviews;
- follow public ticket links;
- use a valid Verified Review Invitation;
- submit the review authorized by that invitation.

## Trust and Authority Moments

| Moment | Audience question | Encore obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public review | Can I trust that this person attended? | Explain the verification level accurately |
| Ticket handoff | Who am I booking with? | Name the provider and preserve clear boundaries |
| Attendance evidence | Why am I eligible? | Use an approved source and allow correction |
| Invitation | What am I authorized to do? | Explain scope, performance, expiry, and single use |
| Submission | Will my honest view be treated fairly? | Apply transparent moderation and publication policy |
| Account offer | Must I join for my review to count? | Make membership optional and non-retrospective |
| Personalisation | Why am I seeing this? | Use permissioned inputs and explain relevant reasons |
| Notification | Why did Encore contact me? | Identify the preference, follow, or consent supporting it |

## Failure and Recovery Journeys

### Attendance Cannot Be Verified

- explain that contribution authority has not been established;
- do not suggest that account creation resolves the problem;
- offer an approved provider, organisation, or Encore correction path where one exists;
- do not lower verification standards merely to avoid journey friction.

### Invitation Is Invalid, Expired, Used, or Mismatched

- provide a safe, non-enumerating explanation;
- preserve the review trust boundary;
- offer support or re-verification where policy permits;
- never grant a generic account-level review permission as a fallback.

### Wrong Performance

- prevent submission under incorrect authority;
- allow source correction through the responsible provider or Encore process;
- retain an audit trail of material correction.

### Review Is Not Published

- show status appropriate to the audience relationship;
- explain the applicable policy without exposing unsafe internal information;
- provide correction or appeal where policy permits;
- ensure the reviewed organisation is not the unchallengeable final authority over legitimate criticism.

### Account Creation Is Declined or Fails

- preserve the valid submitted review;
- do not change verification or publication treatment;
- provide a later path to create My Encore if secure linking is supported.

## Privacy and Control

The journey must distinguish:

- public browsing;
- transaction handoff;
- attendance evidence;
- review authority;
- account identity;
- personalisation;
- analytics;
- direct marketing;
- AI-supported processing.

One choice must not silently authorize another.

Audience members should understand what information is used, why it is used, how long it is retained, and what controls or rights apply.

## Experience Measures

### Discovery

- successful searches and useful result engagement;
- show and venue discovery depth;
- saves, follows, and watchlists;
- diversity and relevance of exposure;
- qualified ticket-provider handoffs.

### Trust

- audience understanding of verification;
- attendance-evidence correction rate;
- invitation validity and completion;
- review status understanding;
- moderation appeal and resolution outcomes;
- trust, complaint, and consent-withdrawal measures.

### Contribution

- verified invitation delivery;
- review completion without forced membership;
- duplicate or invalid authority attempts;
- time and friction from invitation to submission;
- proportion of published content with complete provenance.

### Membership

- optional account conversion after contribution;
- repeat discovery and return use;
- favourites, follows, and watchlist adoption;
- recommendation usefulness;
- preference control and opt-out outcomes.

## Journey Boundaries

Encore must not:

- gate public discovery behind membership;
- treat account identity as review authority;
- require marketing consent for review contribution;
- use booking as universal proof of attendance without an approved rule;
- obscure which provider completes a ticket transaction;
- imply all provider integrations offer equal verification strength;
- prioritize conversion over trust, accessibility, or audience control.

## Assumptions Requiring Validation

- the best point to offer My Encore is after review submission;
- audience members understand and value the difference between identity and authority;
- providers can deliver attendance evidence at an appropriate time and quality;
- review status and appeal visibility increase trust without creating unsafe disclosure;
- favourites, follows, watchlists, and history generate sufficient repeat audience value;
- audiences will accept personalisation when its inputs and controls are visible.
