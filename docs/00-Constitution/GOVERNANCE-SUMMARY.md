# Encore Constitution Governance Summary

Encore exists to orchestrate the live entertainment ecosystem through trusted experiences and collective intelligence.

This document contributes to that purpose.

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Governance summary

**Review date:** 24 July 2026

## Outcome

[The Encore Constitution](CONSTITUTION.md) is now the highest governing authority within the repository. Purpose remains the highest substantive authority within the Constitution.

The Constitution incorporates the Core Purpose, Theory of Change, Manifesto, Trust Promise, Conductor Principle, First Principles and Decision Framework by reference. Existing files remain in place to avoid disruptive moves.

## Governance Hierarchy

```text
Constitution
└── Purpose
    └── Principles
        └── Product
            └── Architecture and ADRs
                └── Engineering
```

Lower layers translate and implement higher layers. They do not redefine them.

## Guardian Model

Product Guardian reviews now validate constitutional, purpose, principle, ADR and Product Specification alignment.

Engineering Guardian reviews validate constitutional architecture, identity/authority separation, trust, provider neutrality, maintainability and orchestration-positive integrations.

## Terminology Decision

The constitutional Purpose uses **collective intelligence**. This is knowledge created from many verified human experiences. **Shared intelligence** remains a valid description of how that collective knowledge is appropriately distributed or applied across the ecosystem, but it is not a competing purpose statement.

## Implementation Boundary

This governance change authorises no application, schema or architecture implementation. Existing code remains untouched.
